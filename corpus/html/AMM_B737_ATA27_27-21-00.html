TASK 27-21-00-400-801 - ELEVATOR FEEL COMPUTER - Removal/Installation

1. Reason for the Job

Replacement of the elevator feel computer as a result of a fault isolation finding.

2. Job Set-up Information

Parts required (see IPC 27-21-00 FIG 1 for effectivity and approved alternates):

PART NUMBERNOMENCLATUREQTYIPC REF
162-23833-14VALVE - SHUTOFF, ELEVATOR FEEL COMPUTER127-21-00 FIG 1

162-30052-14PUMP - TRANSFER, ELEVATOR FEEL COMPUTER127-21-00 FIG 1
60-41635-24HOSE ASSEMBLY, ELEVATOR FEEL COMPUTER227-21-00 FIG 1
WARNING: Do not work on pressurized lines. Injury to persons can occur.
CAUTION: Install protective caps on open lines to prevent contamination.

3. Procedure

  1. Make sure the aircraft is safe for maintenance and the applicable circuit breakers are open and tagged.
  2. Release system pressure and disconnect the lines from the elevator feel computer.
  3. Remove the attaching hardware and remove the VALVE - SHUTOFF, ELEVATOR FEEL COMPUTER.
  4. Install new seals. Lubricate seals with the approved fluid before assembly.
  5. Install the replacement VALVE - SHUTOFF, ELEVATOR FEEL COMPUTER and torque the attaching hardware.
  6. Do the operational test. Examine the connections for leaks.

4. Close-up

Remove tools and tags. Close access panels.
