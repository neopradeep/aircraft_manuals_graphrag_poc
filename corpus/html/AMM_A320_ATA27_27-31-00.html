TASK 27-31-00-400-801 - SPOILER SERVO ACTUATOR - Removal/Installation

1. Reason for the Job

Replacement of the spoiler servo actuator as a result of a fault isolation finding.

2. Job Set-up Information

Parts required (see IPC 27-31-00 FIG 1 for effectivity and approved alternates):

PART NUMBERNOMENCLATUREQTYIPC REF
D27415357100SWITCH - PRESSURE, SPOILER SERVO ACTUATOR427-31-00 FIG 1

D27679265500COUPLING - QUICK DISCONNECT, SPOILER SERVO ACTUATOR227-31-00 FIG 1
WARNING: Do not work on pressurized lines. Injury to persons can occur.
CAUTION: Install protective caps on open lines to prevent contamination.

3. Procedure

  1. Make sure the aircraft is safe for maintenance and the applicable circuit breakers are open and tagged.
  2. Release system pressure and disconnect the lines from the spoiler servo actuator.
  3. Remove the attaching hardware and remove the SWITCH - PRESSURE, SPOILER SERVO ACTUATOR.
  4. Install new seals. Lubricate seals with the approved fluid before assembly.
  5. Install the replacement SWITCH - PRESSURE, SPOILER SERVO ACTUATOR and torque the attaching hardware.
  6. Do the operational test. Examine the connections for leaks.

4. Close-up

Remove tools and tags. Close access panels.
