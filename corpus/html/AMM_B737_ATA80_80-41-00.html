TASK 80-41-00-400-801 - STARTER DUCT - Removal/Installation

1. Reason for the Job

Replacement of the starter duct as a result of a fault isolation finding.

2. Job Set-up Information

Parts required (see IPC 80-41-00 FIG 1 for effectivity and approved alternates):

PART NUMBERNOMENCLATUREQTYIPC REF
162-65448-06SENSOR - PRESSURE, STARTER DUCT280-41-00 FIG 1
162-63626-22WASHER - LOCK, STARTER DUCT280-41-00 FIG 1
65-69942-19DUCT - FLEXIBLE, STARTER DUCT280-41-00 FIG 1
162-27895-07FILTER ELEMENT, STARTER DUCT280-41-00 FIG 1
WARNING: Do not work on pressurized lines. Injury to persons can occur.
CAUTION: Install protective caps on open lines to prevent contamination.

3. Procedure

  1. Make sure the aircraft is safe for maintenance and the applicable circuit breakers are open and tagged.
  2. Release system pressure and disconnect the lines from the starter duct.
  3. Remove the attaching hardware and remove the SENSOR - PRESSURE, STARTER DUCT.
  4. Install new seals. Lubricate seals with the approved fluid before assembly.
  5. Install the replacement SENSOR - PRESSURE, STARTER DUCT and torque the attaching hardware.
  6. Do the operational test. Examine the connections for leaks.

4. Close-up

Remove tools and tags. Close access panels.
