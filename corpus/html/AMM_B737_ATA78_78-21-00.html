TASK 78-21-00-400-801 - THRUST REVERSER SLEEVE - Removal/Installation

1. Reason for the Job

Replacement of the thrust reverser sleeve as a result of a fault isolation finding.

2. Job Set-up Information

Parts required (see IPC 78-21-00 FIG 1 for effectivity and approved alternates):

PART NUMBERNOMENCLATUREQTYIPC REF
60-37762-07ACTUATOR - LINEAR, THRUST REVERSER SLEEVE178-21-00 FIG 1
162-41797-23SENSOR - TEMPERATURE, THRUST REVERSER SLEEVE478-21-00 FIG 1
69-50425-09PUMP - TRANSFER, THRUST REVERSER SLEEVE178-21-00 FIG 1
65-72835-07SEAL - FLANGE, THRUST REVERSER SLEEVE178-21-00 FIG 1
WARNING: Do not work on pressurized lines. Injury to persons can occur.
CAUTION: Install protective caps on open lines to prevent contamination.

3. Procedure

  1. Make sure the aircraft is safe for maintenance and the applicable circuit breakers are open and tagged.
  2. Release system pressure and disconnect the lines from the thrust reverser sleeve.
  3. Remove the attaching hardware and remove the ACTUATOR - LINEAR, THRUST REVERSER SLEEVE.
  4. Install new seals. Lubricate seals with the approved fluid before assembly.
  5. Install the replacement ACTUATOR - LINEAR, THRUST REVERSER SLEEVE and torque the attaching hardware.
  6. Do the operational test. Examine the connections for leaks.

4. Close-up

Remove tools and tags. Close access panels.
