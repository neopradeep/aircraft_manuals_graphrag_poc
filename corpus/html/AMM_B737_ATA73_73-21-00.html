TASK 73-21-00-400-801 - HYDROMECHANICAL UNIT - Removal/Installation

1. Reason for the Job

Replacement of the hydromechanical unit as a result of a fault isolation finding.

2. Job Set-up Information

Parts required (see IPC 73-21-00 FIG 1 for effectivity and approved alternates):

PART NUMBERNOMENCLATUREQTYIPC REF
69-27992-04VALVE - SHUTOFF, HYDROMECHANICAL UNIT473-21-00 FIG 1
60-21725-21BRACKET - MOUNTING, HYDROMECHANICAL UNIT473-21-00 FIG 1
65-16653-22COUPLING - QUICK DISCONNECT, HYDROMECHANICAL UNIT473-21-00 FIG 1
65-10679-18PUMP - TRANSFER, HYDROMECHANICAL UNIT173-21-00 FIG 1
WARNING: Do not work on pressurized lines. Injury to persons can occur.
CAUTION: Install protective caps on open lines to prevent contamination.

3. Procedure

  1. Make sure the aircraft is safe for maintenance and the applicable circuit breakers are open and tagged.
  2. Release system pressure and disconnect the lines from the hydromechanical unit.
  3. Remove the attaching hardware and remove the VALVE - SHUTOFF, HYDROMECHANICAL UNIT.
  4. Install new seals. Lubricate seals with the approved fluid before assembly.
  5. Install the replacement VALVE - SHUTOFF, HYDROMECHANICAL UNIT and torque the attaching hardware.
  6. Do the operational test. Examine the connections for leaks.

4. Close-up

Remove tools and tags. Close access panels.
