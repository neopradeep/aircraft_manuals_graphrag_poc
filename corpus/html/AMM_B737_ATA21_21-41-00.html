TASK 21-41-00-400-801 - CABIN PRESSURE OUTFLOW VALVE - Removal/Installation

1. Reason for the Job

Replacement of the cabin pressure outflow valve as a result of a fault isolation finding.

2. Job Set-up Information

Parts required (see IPC 21-41-00 FIG 1 for effectivity and approved alternates):

PART NUMBERNOMENCLATUREQTYIPC REF
162-17685-06SENSOR - TEMPERATURE, CABIN PRESSURE OUTFLOW VALVE221-41-00 FIG 1

285-96752-23GASKET, CABIN PRESSURE OUTFLOW VALVE221-41-00 FIG 1
65-31798-14CLAMP - V-BAND, CABIN PRESSURE OUTFLOW VALVE221-41-00 FIG 1
WARNING: Do not work on pressurized lines. Injury to persons can occur.
CAUTION: Install protective caps on open lines to prevent contamination.

3. Procedure

  1. Make sure the aircraft is safe for maintenance and the applicable circuit breakers are open and tagged.
  2. Release system pressure and disconnect the lines from the cabin pressure outflow valve.
  3. Remove the attaching hardware and remove the SENSOR - TEMPERATURE, CABIN PRESSURE OUTFLOW VALVE.
  4. Install new seals. Lubricate seals with the approved fluid before assembly.
  5. Install the replacement SENSOR - TEMPERATURE, CABIN PRESSURE OUTFLOW VALVE and torque the attaching hardware.
  6. Do the operational test. Examine the connections for leaks.

4. Close-up

Remove tools and tags. Close access panels.
