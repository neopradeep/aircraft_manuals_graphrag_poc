TASK 27-31-00-400-801 - FLAP DRIVE UNIT - Removal/Installation

1. Reason for the Job

Replacement of the flap drive unit as a result of a fault isolation finding.

2. Job Set-up Information

Parts required (see IPC 27-31-00 FIG 1 for effectivity and approved alternates):

PART NUMBERNOMENCLATUREQTYIPC REF
69-15965-02SENSOR - PRESSURE, FLAP DRIVE UNIT427-31-00 FIG 1

162-74454-03PUMP - TRANSFER, FLAP DRIVE UNIT427-31-00 FIG 1
285-47268-16ACTUATOR - LINEAR, FLAP DRIVE UNIT227-31-00 FIG 1
WARNING: Do not work on pressurized lines. Injury to persons can occur.
CAUTION: Install protective caps on open lines to prevent contamination.

3. Procedure

  1. Make sure the aircraft is safe for maintenance and the applicable circuit breakers are open and tagged.
  2. Release system pressure and disconnect the lines from the flap drive unit.
  3. Remove the attaching hardware and remove the SENSOR - PRESSURE, FLAP DRIVE UNIT.
  4. Install new seals. Lubricate seals with the approved fluid before assembly.
  5. Install the replacement SENSOR - PRESSURE, FLAP DRIVE UNIT and torque the attaching hardware.
  6. Do the operational test. Examine the connections for leaks.

4. Close-up

Remove tools and tags. Close access panels.
