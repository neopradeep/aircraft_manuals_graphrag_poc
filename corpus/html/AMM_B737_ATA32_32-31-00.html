TASK 32-31-00-400-801 - BRAKE ASSEMBLY - Removal/Installation

1. Reason for the Job

Replacement of the brake assembly as a result of a fault isolation finding.

2. Job Set-up Information

Parts required (see IPC 32-31-00 FIG 1 for effectivity and approved alternates):

PART NUMBERNOMENCLATUREQTYIPC REF
162-73656-20ACTUATOR - LINEAR, BRAKE ASSEMBLY232-31-00 FIG 1
162-33028-05SENSOR - TEMPERATURE, BRAKE ASSEMBLY232-31-00 FIG 1
65-43491-10SOLENOID, BRAKE ASSEMBLY132-31-00 FIG 1
285-83233-27COUPLING - QUICK DISCONNECT, BRAKE ASSEMBLY132-31-00 FIG 1
WARNING: Do not work on pressurized lines. Injury to persons can occur.
CAUTION: Install protective caps on open lines to prevent contamination.

3. Procedure

  1. Make sure the aircraft is safe for maintenance and the applicable circuit breakers are open and tagged.
  2. Release system pressure and disconnect the lines from the brake assembly.
  3. Remove the attaching hardware and remove the ACTUATOR - LINEAR, BRAKE ASSEMBLY.
  4. Install new seals. Lubricate seals with the approved fluid before assembly.
  5. Install the replacement ACTUATOR - LINEAR, BRAKE ASSEMBLY and torque the attaching hardware.
  6. Do the operational test. Examine the connections for leaks.

4. Close-up

Remove tools and tags. Close access panels.
